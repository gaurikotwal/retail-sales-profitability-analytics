# Retail Sales & Profitability Analytics

## Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end retail analytics case study focused on analyzing sales performance, profitability, customer behaviour, product performance, and regional trends.

The goal is to transform raw transactional data into meaningful business insights and recommendations using SQL, Power BI, and Python/Pandas.

## Business Objective

The business wants to understand:

* How sales and profit are changing over time
* Which products and categories drive the most revenue and profit
* Which areas of the business are underperforming
* Which customers contribute the most value
* How performance differs across regions
* Where management can improve profitability and sales performance

## Business Questions

1. How are sales and profit trending over time?
2. Which product categories and subcategories generate the most revenue?
3. Which products generate high sales but low or negative profit?
4. Which regions or locations perform best and worst?
5. Who are the highest-value customers?
6. What is the month-over-month sales growth?
7. What percentage of total revenue comes from each product category?
8. Which products and customers consistently rank among the top performers?

## Tools & Technologies

* **Oracle SQL** — data exploration, cleaning, analysis, KPIs, and window functions
* **Microsoft Excel** — data inspection and validation
* **Power BI** — data modelling, DAX, visualization, and dashboard development
* **Python / Pandas** — additional data cleaning and exploratory analysis
* **GitHub** — project documentation and version control

## Project Workflow
